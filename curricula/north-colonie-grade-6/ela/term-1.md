# ELA: Term 1

## Unit 1: Reading Literature

### Lesson 1: Citing textual evidence

- Cite evidence from a story to support an analysis of what it says explicitly.
- Cite evidence to support an inference drawn from a story.

### Lesson 2: Theme

- Determine the theme of a story and explain how particular details convey it.
- Write a summary of a story that leaves out personal opinions.

### Lesson 3: Plot and character

- Describe how a story's plot unfolds in a series of episodes.
- Describe how characters respond or change as the plot moves toward a resolution.

### Lesson 4: Point of view

- Explain how an author develops the point of view of the narrator or speaker.
- Compare a character's point of view with your own.

## Unit 2: Reading Informational Text

### Lesson 1: Central idea

- Determine the central idea of an informational text and how it is conveyed through details.
- Write an objective summary of an informational text.

### Lesson 2: Text structure

- Analyze how a sentence, paragraph or section fits into the overall structure of a text.
- Explain how a section contributes to the development of the ideas.

### Lesson 3: Author's argument

- Trace the argument and specific claims in a text.
- Tell claims supported by reasons and evidence from claims that are not.

## Unit 3: Narrative Writing

### Lesson 1: Planning a narrative

- Establish a context, a point of view and a narrator for a narrative.
- Organize a sequence of events that unfolds naturally.

### Lesson 2: Narrative techniques

- Use dialogue, pacing and description to develop experiences, events and characters.
- Use transition words and phrases to show shifts in time or setting.

### Lesson 3: Revising and concluding

- Write a conclusion that follows from the narrated experiences or events.
- Revise a draft by using precise words and sensory language.

## Unit 4: Language and Vocabulary

### Lesson 1: Pronouns

- Use pronouns in the proper case: subjective, objective and possessive.
- Recognize and correct vague pronouns, whose antecedent is unclear.

### Lesson 2: Word meaning from context

- Use context clues to determine the meaning of an unknown word.
- Use Greek and Latin affixes and roots, such as audience, auditory and audible, as clues to meaning.

### Lesson 3: Figurative language

- Interpret figures of speech, such as personification, in context.
- Distinguish among the connotations of words with similar denotations, such as stingy, economical and thrifty.

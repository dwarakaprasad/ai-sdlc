# Math: Term 1

## Unit 1: Ratios

### Lesson 1: Introducing ratios

- Write a ratio to describe the relationship between two quantities, using a:b, a to b and a/b.
- Use ratio language such as "for every" and "to" to describe a ratio relationship.
- Tell a part-to-part ratio from a part-to-whole ratio.

### Lesson 2: Equivalent ratios

- Find equivalent ratios by multiplying or dividing both quantities by the same number.
- Complete a table of equivalent ratios and use it to compare two ratios.
- Represent equivalent ratios on a double number line.

### Lesson 3: Graphing ratios

- Plot pairs of values from a ratio table on the coordinate plane.
- Explain why the points for equivalent ratios lie on a straight line through the origin.

## Unit 2: Unit Rates and Percents

### Lesson 1: Unit rates

- Explain a unit rate a/b as the amount of the first quantity for 1 of the second.
- Find a unit rate and use it to solve a problem, including unit pricing and constant speed.

### Lesson 2: Converting measurement units

- Use ratio reasoning to convert between measurement units, such as inches and feet.
- Choose whether to multiply or divide when converting, and explain why.

### Lesson 3: Percents

- Explain a percent as a rate per 100.
- Find a percent of a quantity.
- Find the whole when given a part and the percent.

## Unit 3: Dividing Fractions

### Lesson 1: Meanings of division

- Interpret a division as "how many groups?" or "how many in each group?".
- Write a multiplication equation that matches a division equation.

### Lesson 2: Dividing a fraction by a fraction

- Divide a fraction by a fraction using a visual model such as a tape diagram.
- Divide fractions and mixed numbers using multiplication by the reciprocal.

### Lesson 3: Word problems with fraction division

- Solve word problems that involve dividing fractions by fractions.
- Check that an answer is reasonable by estimating.

## Unit 4: Multi-Digit Arithmetic and Factors

### Lesson 1: Dividing multi-digit numbers

- Divide multi-digit whole numbers using the standard algorithm.
- Interpret a remainder in the context of a word problem.

### Lesson 2: Operations with decimals

- Add and subtract multi-digit decimals using the standard algorithm.
- Multiply and divide multi-digit decimals using the standard algorithm.

### Lesson 3: Factors and multiples

- Find the greatest common factor of two whole numbers less than or equal to 100.
- Find the least common multiple of two whole numbers less than or equal to 12.
- Use the distributive property to rewrite a sum, such as 36 + 8 as 4(9 + 2).

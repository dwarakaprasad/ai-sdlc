The Learner's school uses i-Ready Classroom Mathematics. Follow its Try–Discuss–Connect routine: pose a problem, let the Learner try it their own way first, discuss their strategy, then connect it to the method in the Lesson.
Use the models taught in i-Ready before moving to a rule: open number lines and base-ten blocks for place value and addition and subtraction; equal groups, arrays and bar models for multiplication and division.
Ask the Learner to explain their reasoning, not just give an answer, and to check answers by estimating or using the inverse operation.
For multiplication facts, aim for quick recall over the year: by the end of Grade 3 the Learner should know all products of two one-digit numbers from memory.
Keep word problems at a Grade 3 reading level: short sentences, familiar contexts and numbers within 1,000.

# Math: Trimester 1

## Unit 1: Place Value, Addition and Subtraction

### Lesson 1: Place value and rounding

- Read and write three-digit numbers in standard form, word form and expanded form.
- Round a whole number to the nearest 10 using place value or a number line.
- Round a whole number to the nearest 100 using place value or a number line.

### Lesson 2: Adding within 1,000

- Add three-digit numbers using place value strategies, such as adding hundreds, tens and ones
  or using an open number line.
- Add three-digit numbers using the standard algorithm, regrouping when needed.
- Estimate a sum by rounding and use the estimate to check whether an answer is reasonable.

### Lesson 3: Subtracting within 1,000

- Subtract three-digit numbers using place value strategies, such as counting up on an open number line.
- Subtract three-digit numbers using the standard algorithm, including regrouping across a zero.
- Check a difference by adding, and solve one-step word problems with addition and subtraction.

## Unit 2: Multiplication and Division Concepts

### Lesson 1: Understanding multiplication

- Explain a product such as 5 × 7 as the total number of objects in 5 groups of 7 objects each.
- Write a multiplication equation to match a picture of equal groups or an array.

### Lesson 2: Multiplying with arrays and skip-counting

- Build an array to show a multiplication fact and find the product.
- Use skip-counting to multiply by 2, 5 and 10.
- Use the fact that the order of factors does not change the product, such as 3 × 8 = 8 × 3.

### Lesson 3: Multiplication strategies

- Break apart a factor to find a product, such as 4 × 7 = 4 × 5 + 4 × 2.
- Multiply by 0 and by 1, and explain why the products work that way.
- Multiply three numbers by grouping two of them first, such as 3 × 5 × 2 = 3 × 10.

### Lesson 4: Understanding division

- Explain a quotient such as 56 ÷ 8 as the number in each share when 56 objects are shared
  equally into 8 groups.
- Explain a quotient as the number of groups when objects are put into groups of an equal size.
- Write a division equation to match a picture or a sharing situation.

### Lesson 5: Relating multiplication and division

- Solve a division problem by finding the unknown factor, such as finding 32 ÷ 8 by thinking 8 × ? = 32.
- Write the related multiplication and division facts for a fact family.
- Find the unknown number in an equation such as 8 × ? = 48 or 18 ÷ 3 = ?.

### Lesson 6: Multiplication and division word problems

- Solve word problems about equal groups and arrays using multiplication or division.
- Draw a picture or bar model and write an equation with a symbol for the unknown number.
- Explain whether a word problem needs multiplication or division, and why.

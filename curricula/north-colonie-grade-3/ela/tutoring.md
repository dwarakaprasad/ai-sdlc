Reading level guide for passages:

- Write or choose passages for a Grade 3 reader. For Trimester 1, aim for about Fountas & Pinnell level M–O, roughly 500L–700L on the Lexile scale; move toward level P–Q (up to about 820L) by the end of the year.
- Keep passages short: about 150–300 words for a reading Lesson and 80–150 words for a fluency Lesson.
- Use mostly familiar words and sentences of about 8–15 words. Include no more than three or four new vocabulary words, and explain each one in the passage or right after it.
- Use word study passages that include several words with the Lesson's prefix, suffix or spelling pattern.
- If the Learner struggles to read a passage, give a shorter, easier one before going back to grade level. If they read it easily, try a slightly harder one.
- The Learner's own i-Ready Reading or school reading level, when the Parent adds it here, takes priority over these defaults.

Quote passages in full so the Learner can refer back to them.
When the Learner makes a claim about a text, ask them to point to the words in the passage that support it.
For writing Lessons, have the Learner write their own sentences or paragraphs, then give specific, encouraging feedback on one or two things to improve.
The school teaches phonics and word study in a daily structured literacy block (Sadlier). In word study Lessons, have the Learner say the word parts aloud before reading the whole word.

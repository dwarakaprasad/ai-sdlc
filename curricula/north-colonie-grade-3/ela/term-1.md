# ELA: Trimester 1

## Unit 1: Get Set for Literacy

### Lesson 1: Asking and answering questions about a story

- Ask and answer who, what, where, when, why and how questions about a story.
- Point to the words in the story that answer a question.

### Lesson 2: Retelling stories and the central message

- Retell a story in order, including the beginning, middle and end.
- Determine the central message, lesson or moral of a story and explain how the details show it.

### Lesson 3: Characters

- Describe a character's traits, feelings and motivations using details from the story.
- Explain how a character's actions affect what happens next in the story.

### Lesson 4: Point of view

- Tell who is narrating a story.
- Compare their own point of view with the point of view of the narrator or a character.

### Lesson 5: Writing a personal narrative

- Write a narrative about a real experience with a clear beginning, middle and end.
- Use dialogue and descriptions of actions, thoughts and feelings to develop the events.
- Use time order words, such as first, next and finally, to show the order of events.

## Unit 2: Word Study and Fluency

### Lesson 1: Prefixes

- Identify common prefixes, such as un-, re-, pre- and dis-, and tell what they mean.
- Use a prefix to work out the meaning of a new word, such as unhappy or retell.

### Lesson 2: Suffixes

- Identify common suffixes, such as -ful, -less, -ly and -able, and tell what they mean.
- Decode words with common suffixes and tell how the suffix changes the word's meaning.

### Lesson 3: Reading multisyllable words

- Break a multisyllable word into syllables to decode it.
- Read grade-appropriate words with irregular spellings, such as through and enough.

### Lesson 4: Reading fluently

- Read a grade-level passage aloud accurately, at an appropriate pace and with expression.
- Use context to confirm or correct a misread word, rereading when needed.

## Unit 3: Grammar and Conventions

### Lesson 1: Nouns and pronouns

- Identify nouns and pronouns in a sentence and explain what each does.
- Form and use regular and irregular plural nouns, such as boxes, children and mice.

### Lesson 2: Verbs and verb tenses

- Form and use the simple past, present and future tenses of verbs.
- Use regular and irregular past tense verbs, such as walked, ran and ate.
- Make the subject and verb agree in a sentence.

### Lesson 3: Capitals and punctuation

- Capitalize the first word of a sentence, names of people and places, and important words in titles.
- End sentences with the correct punctuation and use commas in addresses and dates.
- Write simple, compound and complex sentences.

## Unit 4: Reading Informational Text

### Lesson 1: Main idea and key details

- Determine the main idea of an informational text.
- Recount the key details and explain how they support the main idea.

### Lesson 2: Text features

- Use text features, such as headings, captions, bold words and a glossary, to find information quickly.
- Explain what a photograph, diagram or map adds to the words of a text.

### Lesson 3: Writing an informative paragraph

- Write an informative paragraph that introduces a topic and groups related facts together.
- Develop the topic with facts, definitions and details, and end with a concluding sentence.

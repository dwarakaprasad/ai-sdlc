## What to build

The Lesson Quiz, up to the Goal being met:
- After the Understanding Check advances, the Tutor generates 10–20 new questions through structured output, scaled to the Lesson's Learning Objectives. Each question has a type (multiple choice, number or short answer), a prompt, an answer key and the objective it covers.
- **Grading:** multiple-choice and number answers are graded by the app; short answers are graded by the LLM.
- **Feedback:** the Learner sees right or wrong, with a one-line explanation, after each answer, and a score at the end of the attempt.
- **Passing:** reaching the pass mark (default 100%) marks the Goal met.
- **Failing:** the Tutor re-teaches only the Learning Objectives that were missed, then starts a new attempt with new questions. After the attempt cap (default 3), the Session ends kindly and the Goal becomes a Flagged Goal.
- **Resuming:** an attempt in progress continues exactly where it stopped.
- The pass mark and attempt cap are adjustable in the Parent's settings.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Quiz attempts and their questions, answers, correctness and score are stored.
- [ ] Multiple-choice and number questions are graded without the LLM. Short answers are graded through structured output.
- [ ] Feedback is shown after each answer and the score at the end of the attempt.
- [ ] Passing marks the Goal met.
- [ ] Failing triggers re-teaching of only the missed objectives, then a new attempt with different questions.
- [ ] Reaching the attempt cap makes the Goal a Flagged Goal.
- [ ] Leaving mid-attempt and coming back resumes the same attempt at the next unanswered question.
- [ ] The Parent can change the pass mark and attempt cap.
- [ ] Seam 1 tests cover passing first time, failing then passing, reaching the attempt cap, resuming mid-Quiz, and a non-default pass mark.

## What to build

The first Tutor Session from end to end:
- Tapping a Goal card starts a Session, or resumes the open one.
- The Tutor module runs the first part of the teaching steps:
  - **Explanation** of the Lesson's Learning Objectives at the Learner's grade level, shaped by the Subject's Tutoring Instructions.
  - **Understanding Check**: a conversational Q&A. A structured-output verdict decides between advancing and re-explaining.
  - Each re-explanation tries a different approach. After the re-explanation cap (default 3), the Goal becomes a Flagged Goal.
  - Advancing leaves the Session ready for the Lesson Quiz, which a later ticket builds.
- Tutor replies stream to the browser over Server-Sent Events. Maths renders with KaTeX.
- The Tutor's instructions keep it on the current Lesson, use age-appropriate language, and never ask for personal information.
- Every message is stored as the Session transcript.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Starting a Session on a Goal produces a streamed Explanation. Leaving and coming back resumes the same Session at the same point.
- [ ] The Understanding Check verdict, from a structured LLM output, either advances to "ready for quiz" or triggers a re-explanation.
- [ ] Reaching the re-explanation cap (configurable, default 3) marks the Goal as a Flagged Goal and ends the Session kindly.
- [ ] Tutor instructions include the Lesson's Learning Objectives, the Tutoring Instructions, the Learner's grade, and the guardrails.
- [ ] Every Learner and Tutor message is stored with its Session.
- [ ] The Learner chat UI streams replies and renders maths with KaTeX.
- [ ] Seam 1 tests with the scripted fake provider cover: advancing, re-explaining and then advancing, reaching the cap and becoming a Flagged Goal, and resuming.
